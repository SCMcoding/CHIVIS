The Rose Tarriot Organ

Step inside a richly detailed virtual organ chamber, featuring a monumental pipe organ, sculpted pipework, luminous floor clock, coloured tiles, and grand silver doors. Explore the room from cinematic viewpoints or fly freely through the architecture in Bird mode.

Play the organ using its on-screen manuals, pedals, and drawknob stops, or listen to a library of 40 classical organ works. Shape the presentation with ambient camera movements, optional door-opening scenes, theatrical mist vents, coloured spotlights, and adjustable room lighting. The simulator also supports compatible MIDI keyboards for hands-on performance.

How To Use

Press Play beneath the preview, then Play on the organ’s title screen to enter the chamber. Use the top menu to open Views, Music, Building, and additional settings.
