Leviathan — Into the Maelstrom

Ride a sea-serpent-themed rollercoaster above a vast whirlpool, surrounded by misty mountains, ancient temples, ruined towers, and stone monuments. The opening cinematic tours the sanctuary before the Leviathan emerges from the water.

Take the front seat for steep climbs and drops, follow the train in third person, or orbit the serpent’s head. The Leviathan swims through the ocean, dives beneath the surface, and occasionally rises beside the train to unleash a blast of water. Rain, ocean ambience, coaster rumble, and creature sounds accompany the ride. Switch to Bird explorer to fly freely through the landscape and explore the ancient ruins.

How To Use

Press Play below the preview, then press Play on the game’s title screen. Click inside the game before using keyboard controls. Choose Front seat to ride or Bird explorer to explore.
