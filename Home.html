Description

SYSTEM NAME
Observational Research Archival Cultural Learning Engine (ORACLE)
PURPOSE

The Observational Research Archival Cultural Learning Engine, also known as ORACLE, is an engine specifically designed to research and explore puppet cultures from around the world. It allows anyone to experience and learn about these cultures through a guided tour or through traditional music.

RESEARCH SYSTEM

ORACLE relies on existing sources to gather information about puppetry traditions, using keywords to locate relevant history, cultural information, locations, images, time periods, and music. The system was developed through prompt engineering, visual design, and HTML-based programming.

PLEASE NOTE

Please Note: ORACLE is an experimental program and may occasionally experience bugs, errors, missing information, or unexpected behavior. Because the system relies on external online sources, some content may also change or become temporarily unavailable.

04 / ACCESS THE HIDDEN THINKER PROGRAM

THINKER is a hidden research mode within ORACLE. To access it, first turn ORACLE OFF using the power control.

While the system is OFF, click inside ORACLE and press the T key four times. The first press activates the hidden signal. The next three presses must be completed within five seconds to reveal the password screen.
